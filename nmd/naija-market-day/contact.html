Questions

Is entry to the markets free? +

Yes. Naija Market Day is free and open to the public.

How do I become a vendor? +

Create a vendor account, upload your regulatory information, and the NMD team reviews it. Once approved you can list products and book a booth.

How do I pay for a booth? +

Pay by card with Stripe in GBP, USD or CAD, then choose your position on the market map.

Can I buy on Naija Market Day? +

Browse the Market Square with no account. Create a free account to chat with sellers. Payment is arranged directly with the seller.

Does NMD take payment for goods? +

No. We connect buyers and sellers. We earn from booth fees and ads.
